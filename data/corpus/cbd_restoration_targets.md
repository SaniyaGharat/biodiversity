# Section: Section H - Target 2: Ecosystem Restoration Benchmarks (p. 9)

Under Target 2 of the Kunming-Montreal Global Biodiversity Framework (Decision CBD/COP/15/L.25, Section H, p. 9), Parties committed that by 2030, at least 30 percent of areas of degraded terrestrial, inland water, and coastal and marine ecosystems are under effective restoration. Effective restoration mandates the recovery of ecological integrity, enhancement of ecosystem services (such as carbon sequestration and water purification), and the re-establishment of ecological connectivity across fragmented production landscapes.

Restoration strategies must prioritize native species assemblages, ecological corridors, and degraded high-biodiversity ecosystems rather than establishing single-species commercial plantations.

# Section: Section H - Target 3 & Target 10: 30x30 Conservation and Agroecology (pp. 9-11)

Target 3 mandates the effective conservation and equitable management of at least 30 percent of terrestrial, inland water, and marine areas globally by 2030 through protected area networks and Other Effective Area-Based Conservation Measures (OECMs) (Section H, p. 10).

Target 10 requires that areas under agriculture, aquaculture, fisheries, and forestry are managed sustainably, in particular through the substantial scaling of biodiversity-friendly practices. This encompasses agroecological approaches, conservation tillage, continuous cover cropping, integrated pest management, and the enhancement of landscape heterogeneity through field margins and vegetative corridors (Section H, p. 11).
