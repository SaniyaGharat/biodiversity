# Section: Target 2 - Ecosystem Restoration Benchmarks

Target 2 of the Kunming-Montreal Global Biodiversity Framework establishes that by 2030, at least 30 percent of areas of degraded terrestrial, inland water, and coastal and marine ecosystems must be under effective restoration. Effective ecological restoration requires recovering biodiversity integrity, enhancing ecosystem functions and services, and restoring ecological connectivity across modified landscapes.

Restoration efforts should prioritize high-biodiversity areas, degraded primary habitats, and critical ecological corridors. Interventions must employ native species assemblages, avoiding single-species commercial monocultures which fail to restore complex food webs or soil biological functions.

# Section: Target 3 & Target 10 - Protected Areas and Sustainable Agroecosystems

Target 3 mandates the conservation and management of at least 30 percent of terrestrial and inland water areas through ecologically representative, well-connected systems of protected areas and other effective area-based conservation measures (OECMs).

Target 10 specifically directs that areas under agriculture, aquaculture, fisheries, and forestry are managed sustainably, in particular through the substantial increase of biodiversity-friendly practices. This includes the widespread adoption of agroecology, regenerative soil management, integrated pest management, and the enhancement of landscape heterogeneity through field margins, hedgerows, and multi-species agroforestry systems.
